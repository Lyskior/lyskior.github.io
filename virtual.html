Featured Project: Virtual Tour & Admissions Chatbot

This project combines an interactive virtual school tour with a chatbot designed to help prospective students and families navigate information more easily. I built it in response to a real problem I noticed: students and parents new to the school often struggle to understand the campus layout and find answers to practical questions.

  • Designed and implemented the overall system and interface
  • Focused on usability, navigation, and clear user experience
  • Connected the project to a real admissions-related need
  • Combined spatial navigation with chatbot-based information access
