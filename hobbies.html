What They Have in Common

These hobbies look different, but they all combine control and creativity. FPV drones demand precision and reaction, guitar depends on rhythm and expression, and games reward strategy and adaptation. Together, they give me ways to focus, experiment, improve, and enjoy the process of getting better.

Focus Practice Creativity Adaptation

Different activities, connected by the same drive to improve.

Technical + instinctive

FPV Drones

FPV drones combine control, reaction time, hardware, camera systems, and spatial awareness. I like how flying feels both technical and instinctive: every movement requires precision, but smooth flying eventually becomes almost automatic.

Control Reaction time Hardware Spatial awareness

Building control through flying, practice, and hardware.

Rhythm + expression

Electric Guitar

Guitar gives me a creative outlet away from school and technical projects. It teaches rhythm, patience, tone, and the satisfaction of slow improvement. Small changes in timing and touch can completely change how a song feels.

Rhythm Patience Tone Expression

A creative outlet away from screens and schoolwork.

Mechanics + decisions

Video Games

I enjoy games that involve mechanics, timing, teamwork, and decision-making. They are a way to relax while still thinking strategically, adapting to other players, and learning from mistakes in real time.

Strategy Timing Teamwork Adaptation

Strategy, timing, and mechanics in action.
